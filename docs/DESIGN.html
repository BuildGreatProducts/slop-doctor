Slop Doctor

A light, Swiss-grid design system. Clean paper-white surfaces, one saturated orange, Switzer for words and Geist Mono for figures, flat tonal depth, architectural corners, and a pencil-line doctor in the margins. Adopted from the Product Squad system; tokens unchanged.

Do's and Don'ts

  • ✓Ration primary to one element per screen. Reached rungs count as that element on the ladder.
  • ✓Set every number in Geist Mono with tabular figures.
  • ✓Draw structure with hairlines and spacing before reaching for a card.
  • ✓Keep the pencil graphite. Agents and doodles are never orange.
  • ✓Use the hatch for "not yet"; the paper grid only in the header band, empty states and the hero.
  • ✓Put black text on orange.
  • ✗No dark mode, purple or cyan accent, gradient, glow or glass card.
  • ✗No corners above 8px outside a dialog. Never round a rung.
  • ✗No orange as body text. Use primary-deep if unavoidable.
  • ✗No second accent and no yellow. Yellow is Skool.
  • ✗No Inter, Instrument Serif, Outfit or Plus Jakarta Sans in place of Switzer.
  • ✗No shadows for hierarchy; no paper grid behind body text, tables or forms.
